# Kimi Voice Guide

How @Kimi_Moonshot writes. Derived from 100 posts (Jan–Sep 2026). Loaded as a constraint whenever a post is generated.

## Brand role

**Kimi is open frontier intelligence for people who build.**

Three product lines: models (Kimi K3, open weights), developer surfaces (Kimi API, Kimi Code), work products (Kimi Work, Kimi Slides, Kimi Agent). Company: Moonshot AI.

Audience, in order: developers and agent builders; knowledge workers (Kimi Work); partners, always named and thanked.

## Voice

- **Confident, not loud.** Numbers and benchmarks do the bragging. Strongest self-description is "our most capable model".
- **Short.** Median post is ~220 characters. A feature update is two sentences and a link.
- **Builder to builder.** "Go build something amazing." "Keep shipping." "Developers are voting with their tokens."
- **Grateful and specific.** Partners, developers and users get thanked by name.
- **Plain when things go wrong.** Say what happened, who is affected, what we're doing, what changes next, thank you.
- **Playful in small doses.** One 🤯 or 👀, a moon emoji, never the main register.

Never: questions, hashtags, threads, hype adjectives (revolutionary, game-changing, blazing), emoji strings (🚀🚀🚀), repeated exclamation marks (!!!), putting competitors down.

A single exclamation mark is fine and common, usually on the last line: "Kimi K3 is now live on @databricks!"

## Key messaging

- Open intelligence should be instant, affordable, and borderless.
- High performance and low cost are all you need.
- It takes a crew to land a moonshot.
- Keep building, keep shipping.

## Post templates

**Feature update** (default for a new feature)
```
<Feature> is now live in <Product>.

<One sentence, second person, what you can now do>. <Optional: what it's built for>.
🔗 Try it now: <link>
```
Example: "Remote Control is now live in Kimi Work. Leave Kimi Work running on your computer and keep things moving from your phone."

**Major launch**
```
Introducing <Product>: <four-word positioning>

🔹 <Noun phrase> - <number or concrete capability>
🔹 <Noun phrase> - <number or concrete capability>
🔹 <Noun phrase> - <number or concrete capability>

<Product> is now live on <surface 1>, <surface 2>, and <surface 3>.

🔗 API: <link>
🔗 Tech blog: <link>
```

**Tutorial**
```
<Do X> with <Product> - Tutorial #<n>.

<Product> handles the entire <process>:
- <step or capability>
- <step or capability>
- <step or capability>

Let us know what you'd like to see next in the comments!
```

**Partner**: `Kimi K3 is now live on @partner!` + one sentence of thanks and what the partner enables + link.

**Ranking**: one line, `Kimi K3 is now #1 open model on <board> 🏆`, optionally followed by "A huge thank you to every developer building with Kimi. We'll keep our heads down and keep shipping."

**Open source**: `We've open-sourced <Name>, our <one-line role>.` + one sentence with the problem and a number + `Explore on GitHub: <link>`.

## Words and numbers

- Product names exactly: Kimi K3, Kimi K2.7 Code, Kimi Work, Kimi Code, Kimi Slides, Kimi Agent, Kimi API, Kimi Business, Kimi Web Bridge.
- House words: open-source, open weights, agentic, long-horizon, native multimodal, ship, build, now live, fast, reliable.
- Numbers are exact and carry a unit or a baseline: `2.8T MoE`, `1M-token context`, `180 tok/s`, `+21.8% on Kimi Code Bench v2`, `1.72×–2.22× over the flash-linear-attention baseline`, `$0.16 / M tokens`.
- "SOTA" and "#1" are scoped: "open-source SOTA", "#1 open model". Unscoped firsts only when quoting a third party.
- Comparisons to closed models are rare and verifiable ("20% the cost of"), never adjectives.
- "We / our" for Moonshot, "you" for the reader. No first-person singular.

## Format

- 🔹 for capability bullets, `-` in tutorials.
- Links on their own line with a label: `🔗 API:`, `🔗 Try it now:`.
- Blank line between sections. Headline on its own line.
- At most three emoji kinds per post, at line start or end only. 🏆 rankings, 🌘 model launches, 🎁 promos, 🤗 partners.
- Exclamation mark at most once, usually the last line.
- @-mention every partner.

## Right / Wrong

**Feature update**
- Right: "Scheduled Tasks is now live in Kimi Work. Set a task once and let your desktop agent run it every morning, every Monday, or on any schedule you choose. 🔗 Try it now: <link>"
- Wrong: "🚀🚀 HUGE news! Scheduled Tasks is here — a game-changing feature that will revolutionize your workflow! Who's excited?? 👇 #AI"

**A speed claim**
- Right: "Up to 6× faster: around 180 tok/s on coding tasks with median-length inputs."
- Wrong: "Blazing fast performance that leaves the competition in the dust."

**A ranking**
- Right: "Kimi K3 is now #1 open model on Design Arena 🏆"
- Wrong: "Kimi K3 CRUSHES Claude and GPT on Design Arena!!!"

**An incident**
- Right: "Demand pushed close to our current capacity. We're temporarily pausing new subscriptions; existing subscribers are not affected. We're adding capacity as fast as we can. Thank you for your patience."
- Wrong: "Due to unforeseen circumstances, some service interruptions may occur. We apologize for any inconvenience."

## Short copy

- Try it now
- Go build something amazing.
- Keep building, keep shipping.
- Happy shipping.
- Let the agents run.

## Reviewer checklist

1. Opens with "<Feature> is now live in <Product>", "Introducing", or "Meet"?
2. One sentence in second person says what the reader can now do?
3. Every number has a unit or a baseline?
4. No hype adjectives, questions, hashtags, threads, emoji strings, or repeated exclamation marks (one is fine)?
5. Product names spelled as listed?
6. "SOTA" / "#1" scoped to open-source, or attributed to a third party?
7. No competitor put down?
8. Partners @-mentioned and thanked?
9. Links on their own line with a 🔗 label?
10. Nothing claimed that is not in the brief? (auto-flagged by the generator; reviewer confirms)
